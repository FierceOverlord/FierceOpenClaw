# 10kg Weight Gain Diet Plan

**Goal:** Gain 10 kg of lean body mass  
**Timeline:** Approximately 20–30 weeks (0.3–0.5 kg per week)  
**Approach:** Moderate caloric surplus + resistance training + consistency

---

## 1. Calories & Surplus

To gain weight, you must eat in a **caloric surplus** — consuming more energy than you burn.

| Parameter | Recommendation |
|-----------|----------------|
| Maintenance calories | Estimate using TDEE calculator (age, sex, weight, height, activity level) |
| Daily surplus | +300 to +500 kcal above maintenance |
| Example daily target | **2,800 – 3,200 kcal** for most adults (adjust based on your TDEE) |
| Weekly weight gain target | 0.3 – 0.5 kg |

> **Note:** If you are not gaining weight after 2 weeks, increase daily intake by another 200–300 kcal.

---

## 2. Macronutrient Targets

| Macro | Grams per kg body weight | Example (70 kg person) | % of calories |
|-------|--------------------------|------------------------|---------------|
| **Protein** | 1.6 – 2.2 g/kg | 112 – 154 g | 25–30% |
| **Carbohydrates** | 4 – 7 g/kg | 280 – 490 g | 45–55% |
| **Fats** | 0.8 – 1.2 g/kg | 56 – 84 g | 20–30% |

- **Protein** supports muscle repair and growth. Spread intake across 3–5 meals.
- **Carbohydrates** fuel training and replenish glycogen; prioritize complex carbs with some simple carbs around workouts.
- **Fats** support hormone health; include omega-3 and monounsaturated sources.

## 4. Calorie-Boosting Tips

If you struggle to hit your calorie target:

1. **Drink your calories** — smoothies, shakes, and whole milk are easier to consume than large meals.
2. **Add healthy fats** — drizzle olive oil on vegetables, add avocado to sandwiches, stir nut butter into oatmeal.
3. **Eat more frequently** — aim for 4–6 eating occasions per day.
4. **Choose calorie-dense foods** — nuts, seeds, dried fruit, whole-fat dairy, fatty fish, olive oil.
5. **Don’t skip carbs around workouts** — they fuel performance and recovery.

---

## 5. Training & Lifestyle Recommendations

| Area | Guidance |
|------|----------|
| **Resistance training** | 3–5 days per week; focus on compound lifts (squat, deadlift, press, row) with progressive overload |
| **Cardio** | Limit to 2–3 short sessions per week (20–30 min) to avoid burning excess calories |
| **Sleep** | 7–9 hours nightly — muscle grows during rest |
| **Hydration** | 3–4 liters of water daily |
| **Consistency** | Eat at your surplus target every day, including weekends |

---

## 6. Tracking & Adjustments

- **Weigh yourself** once per week, same day and time (fasted, morning).
- **Take progress photos** every 2–4 weeks.
- **If gaining too fast** (>0.7 kg/week): reduce daily intake by 200 kcal.
- **If not gaining** after 2 weeks: increase daily intake by 200–300 kcal.
- **Prioritize lean gains** — aim for muscle, not just scale weight.
